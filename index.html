Why this helps the grid

0 kWh

On-peak hours are when Ontario uses the most power. Ontario's grid operator says natural gas plants are among the flexible generators that are called on to meet peak demand, and Save on Energy says gas plants are the single biggest source of air emissions on the Ontario grid. Every kWh moved to off-peak or overnight lowers that peak.
